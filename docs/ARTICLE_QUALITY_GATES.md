# Article quality gates: source fidelity, formal validity, human prose, owner release

These requirements apply to every Sweet Rationalism article audit and derived edition. Automated pattern matching is not an AI-authorship detector; automated proof checking is not peer review. Protected article text and its private audits must not enter public GitHub or external services before release.

## Source and citation ledger

For each load-bearing claim, record its exact quoted wording, section/edition, claim type, quantifier, boundary, timescale, qualification and stated loss conditions. Pair it with the cited work's verified author/title/year/DOI/version, publication and retraction status, *actual* relevant passage (page/section/equation/table), experimental design and limitations, and whether the source **establishes, is compatible with, motivates, challenges, or fails to support** the attributed claim. Record any further bridge premise, counterinterpretation, dependency, revision, and retrieval/access limits.

Check every bibliography entry, inline citation, borrowed equation, quotation and cited numerical finding against the original work, not merely its DOI or abstract. Prefer full peer-reviewed papers and their supplements; retain preprint/final-version differences and errata. Treat reused studies, repeated summaries, and correlated AI outputs as one lineage, not independent replications. Do not invent sources or quotations. Keep unverified sources clearly flagged.

Use the [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/) and [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/) to check philosophical concepts and controversies. Mathematical definitions and scientific quantities must also be checked against original mathematical/technical sources or recognized scholarly textbooks. Encyclopedia exposition does not validate any particular ontology.

## Single final reference list gate

Every audited long-form article must have one unified `References` section at the end of the article, following all substantive appendices and revision notes unless an applicable scholarly style specifically requires another placement. Integrate all verified sources, including references found in historical revision sections, into subject-appropriate places in that one list. Do not strand an `Additional References` list between appendices, or hide a second bibliography inside revision notes. Preserve every distinct directly bearing source, original paper quote, inline citation, and supplied dialogue quote; preserve the historical editions intact in an immutable revision archive. Deduplicate only source-identical works, comparing DOI, version and publication identity. Require a machine-checkable before/after DOI identity-set receipt, reference-inventory reconciliation, source-to-claim check, section-order and rendering readback, and a manually adjudicated quote-retention ledger. Passing a DOI-set check is not proof of accurate scholarly attribution.

Completion gate: **`[ ] All appendices and revision notes precede one unified references list, all distinct sources/quotes retained or explicitly adjudicated, identity reconciliation and rendered readback passed.`**

## Opening Bottom line gate: required for every completed article audit

An article is not fully audited until its **near-top `Bottom line.` passage** has passed both a structural placement check and an editorial/source check. Place the short reader-facing statement **immediately after any opening `Research record`, `Cite as`, and `Versions and archives` metadata** (in that order if present; otherwise after the title/deck if such metadata is legitimately absent), before the abstract, prologue, first substantive section or extended setup. A real citation/license paragraph is opening provenance metadata, **not a substantive introduction**: the structural gate must allow even a long `Cite as` paragraph before `Bottom line.` If the citation or archives metadata is present, placing it after `Bottom line.` is a placement failure. Keep matching version/concept DOI badges, accessibility text and archive hyperlinks intact during any move. The preferred precedent is Nathan Sweet's *Michael Levin’s Platonic Morphospace: The Ingression Challenge and Its Evidence*: it affirms the biological experiments, identifies Douglas Brash's particular challenge to **ingression**, asks what unique observation would favor that added cause over physical/developmental/organizational rivals, and explicitly refuses to promote a failure of one causal account into a refutation of all Platonism.

For each new article's opening, a human reviewer must be able to identify:

1. **Target and conclusion:** the exact proposed claim and strongest warranted bottom-line answer, without an unjustified universal quantifier.
2. **Positive evidence and rival:** the observation or argument that actually survives, the most serious relevant alternative and the inferential bridge that has or has not been earned.
3. **Scholarly retrieval anchors:** named scholars, canonical paper titles or terms of art genuinely used in that article, accurately attributed and linked to verified primary papers, DOIs or SEP/IEP definitions where helpful. For example, a discussion of empirical immunization might naturally invoke **Antony Flew**, **Popperian falsificationism**, **Lakatos**, and **Deborah Mayo's severe testing**; a consciousness-substitution article might instead foreground **Kleiner and Hoel**. Never force all these names into unrelated articles, replace arguments with keyword lists, or claim a measurable SEO/computational-discovery gain without evidence.
4. **Scope and correction:** clarify what follows and what remains undecided, including what contrary evidence or argument would change the assessment. Applied especially symmetrically to **claims of exhaustive physicalism** (not entailed by successful physical methodology) and **anti-physicalist/supernaturalist appeals to gaps** (not confirmed by a rival's limitations).
5. **Readable prose:** normally one compact paragraph near 80–160 words, but allow shorter or slightly longer where content earns it. No canned `not X but Y` treadmill, artificial triads, grandiose claim of definitive victory, or duplicated abstract.

Run `python3 scripts/bottom_line_gate.py ARTICLE.html --format html` on the **complete candidate body** and keep its receipt; a failed structural check blocks audit closure. Independently judge source fidelity, scholarly relevance and inferential fairness. A passing automated placement check only proves the phrase was found in the configured opening region. Final checklist item: **`[ ] Bottom line present, near-top, reader-tested, scholar/definition/source-linked where warranted, scope-correct, and independently reviewed.`**

## Formal mathematics, computation and logic gate

Create one proof-obligation record per theorem, lemma, inequality, algorithm, logical inference, statistical claim and quantitative model:

1. **Claim status:** distinguish established theorem, derived corollary, full new proof, informal sketch, conjecture, simulation, analogy and empirical hypothesis. Never call a sketch or numerical test a proof.
2. **Contract:** declare symbols, types, domains/codomains, quantifiers/order, logical system and semantics, physical units/dimensions, temperature where needed, boundary and time grain, data, measure, topology, algorithm, initial conditions and tolerances. Require definitions for all terms in a purported theorem.
3. **Source alignment:** verify original theorem assumptions, actual equations, proof steps, regularity conditions, scope and prior work. For a category/functor define objects, morphisms, identity, composition and prove preservation. For thermodynamics distinguish a stored bit, logical irreversibility, erasure, measurement, entropy production and power.
4. **Adversarial checks:** inspect empty/singleton/degenerate/symmetric/limiting models, quantifier reversals, counterexamples, infeasible substitutions, measure dependence and omitted variables. One valid admissible countermodel defeats a universal statement in the specified domain.
5. **Executable checks:** use exact arithmetic, dimensional analysis, symbolic algebra, enumeration, SAT/SMT satisfiability, property testing, seeded simulation, numerical sensitivity and kernel-checked theorem proofs when suitable. For logic, test premises AND negated conclusion for satisfiability. Document the translation and verify it independently; a solver cannot know whether the formalization matches the prose.
   - For explicitly translated *classical propositional* arguments, make a private JSON claim ledger modeled on `examples/logic_claims_example.json` and run `python3 scripts/logic_audit.py LEDGER.json`. Retain the generated countermodels and human verification of the translation. The dependency-free tool exhaustively checks up to 12 propositional atoms by default and separately detects inconsistent premises. It cannot check first-order, modal, mathematical, causal or natural-language validity. For these use appropriate logics, explicit models, SAT/SMT encodings or proof assistants and keep all bridging assumptions visible.
6. **Reproducibility:** save code, environment versions/lockfiles, source hashes, commands, inputs, seeds, outputs, adverse cases, runtimes and missing packages. Two differently implemented checks are preferable for consequential claims; repeated runs of one library are correlated, not independent verification.
7. **Empirical bridge:** separately test whether premises, measurements and causal interpretation hold in the intended physical system. Pure mathematical validity does not establish phenomenality, necessity, universal identity or another empirical/ontological conclusion.
8. **Rivals:** compare with the strongest available peer-reviewed mathematical constructions and lower-commitment alternatives, including what they do *not* establish. Do not claim novelty until the relevant prior art has been searched.

Use exact status labels: `proved by a complete derivation`; `kernel-checked under specified assumptions`; `SMT unsatisfiable under encoding`; `counterexample`; `bounded tests passed`; `dimensional mismatch`; `proof incomplete`; `empirical bridge unverified`. A solver's UNSAT result supports only that encoding and solver theory. Passing finitely many tests does not prove an unrestricted theorem.

Definitional anchors: [SEP Classical Logic](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logic-classical/) for semantic consequence and countermodels; [IEP Validity and Soundness](https://iep.utm.edu/val-snd/) for formal validity versus true premises; [SEP Category Theory](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2026/entries/category-theory/) for categories and functor laws. Check context-specific scholarly sources for every technical application.

## Human-readable prose gate

Run `python3 scripts/prose_audit.py ARTICLE.html --format html --json` on a private copy of the candidate, then perform a full human edit and selected read-aloud/TTS inspection. The script produces prompts for judgment and does not rewrite or score authorship.

Inspect recurring structures *within and across sections*, especially:

- **Antithetical treadmill:** `not X but Y`, `not only ... but also`, `not merely`, reversed `X rather than Y` and repeated `the question is not` constructions. Keep contrasts that do real analytic work.
- **Power-of-three reflex:** three adjectives, three-part lists, three rhetorical questions, tricolons and three-stage conclusions imposed for cadence. Preserve genuine three-way taxonomies, theorem cases and experimental groups.
- **Canned transitions:** `the deeper point`, `importantly`, `crucially`, `in other words`, `this is where`, `ultimately`, `what matters is`, `to be clear` and their excessive reuse.
- **Over-signposting:** repeated section roadmaps, ritual methodological declarations, caveats and miniature conclusions replacing argument.
- **Uniform rhythm and layout:** repetitive sentence starts, serial one-line punch sentences, symmetrical clauses, em-dash saturation, excessive colons, near-identical paragraph lengths, heading density and bold/bullet overload.
- **Decorative fluency:** generic `delve`/`tapestry`/`landscape`/`nuance`/`robust` vocabulary, abstract noun stacks, unearned intensifiers and vague source attributions.
- **Obscured reasoning:** slogan where an equation or premise belongs, rhetorical questions that assume their answer, citations with no explained relevance, repeated accusations, near-duplicate paragraphs, and unexplained abbreviations.

Classify each flag as `edit`, `retain with reason`, or `investigate`. A legitimate binary distinction, necessary three-case proof, precise quotation, repeated statutory term, deliberate joke and recurring corrective caveat can be warranted. Never mechanically remove all examples of a style feature. Preserve Nathan's natural directness, strong earned criticism, humor, exact debate quotations, evidence, premises, uncertainty and ethical force. Reread every changed passage and compare the exact diff for altered scope or lost inferential bridges.


## Mandatory whole-article argument map and evidence-preserving compression

Run **two independent editorial passes**: (1) each section independently; (2) the entire article continuously from opening through references, with cross-section claim clustering. Each section must have a distinct role in a map recording `section ID, proposition IDs, evidence/quoted material, premises, inference, rivals, harms, loss conditions, next-section dependency`. A repetition checker is a lead only: two different phrasings of the same inference can evade lexical similarity, while exact repeated quotations may be required evidence.

For every proposition repeated in distant sections, ask whether the later occurrence adds a new premise, evidence, objection, boundary, reply, risk or test. If yes, preserve that additional work, possibly by moving it into one complete analysis and using a short cross-reference. If no, trim or merge. Inspect section boundaries and consider merging neighboring headings that share one argumentative function. Record `old sections, exact passage spans, proposed destination, evidence/quotes retained, claims retained, distinct risks retained, decision and justification` for **every** proposed move/deletion. Protect relevant peer-reviewed references and verified quotations from those works; also protect user-supplied personal and public dialogue quotations and their context, attribution and publication status. Flag any uncertain quotation for re-verification instead of silently deleting or publishing it. Compare the complete old and new article for lost premises, source associations, rebuttals, conclusions, limitations, harms, corrections and voice.

Assess the *whole document* for reader navigation, logical ordering, heading semantics, an honest abstract versus later proof, repeated methodology disclaimers, disproportionate appendix weight, total reading load, consecutive sentence openings, transitions, passive voice, excessive paragraph/sentence lengths, unexplained jargon, Flesch suitability and Yoast’s actual feedback. **Never add canned transition words, shorten technical proofs, or remove a necessary caveat just to turn a Yoast light green.** Rerun heuristic detectors on the full revised master and perform a human read-aloud review across section boundaries. Complete only when the full-article coverage matrix and defended `merge | trim | move | retain | unresolved` log exist.

## Sitewide internal-link and search-intent gate

Inventory **all currently published, indexable public articles** and applicable public framework/topic hubs at the audit date. Cross-check WordPress status against Yoast robots/noindex, the independent unlisting plugin, canonical URLs, index inventory, and actual accessibility; `publish` alone does not make a page eligible. For each eligible source, record a decision about linking to the audited article: `direct connection | loose connection | no useful connection`, with `link | defer | reject`, exact original context, destination canonical URL/section, the specific new explanatory benefit, topical search intent, suggested descriptive anchor text and reason/verification date. Inventory relevant outbound links from the audited article as well.

For a **direct connection**, add a small, naturally placed explanatory sentence identifying the related article and what it argues, tests, corrects or supplies that the current passage does not. For a **loose connection**, attach a descriptive anchor to existing accurate prose if a reader would genuinely benefit. Otherwise retain `no link`. Select anchors for accurate reader expectations and relevant search queries; use varied normal language and verified Search Console/Yoast data where present. Google Search Central recommends crawlable `<a href>` links with informative natural text; avoid keyword stuffing, repetitive exact-match anchors, irrelevant reciprocals and promises of ranking gains. Source pages must remain useful independently. Check both directions because article discovery is an argument navigation problem rather than a link-count target.

**Hard privacy gate:** For a protected/new article awaiting explicit owner-approved DOI and indexing release, proposed public inbound links remain in the **private** release plan; do not edit indexed articles to expose it. After release approval, recheck all 36-or-current public targets, the newly public target, anchor accuracy, redirects, signed-out rendering, link response, Yoast and canonical/noindex status. No article is fully audited without a per-source `link/defer/reject` matrix and tested approved changes. Record negative and out-of-scope candidates for Gemini/Antigravity.

## Hostile peer-review coverage gate

For each substantive inference, build a private `claim -> source passage -> evidential type -> premise(s) -> conclusion -> rival -> counterexample/replication -> disposition -> loss condition` record. Check **every** directly bearing peer-reviewed citation, quoted source passage and supplied dialogue excerpt for identity and context, preserving them in the article or exact separately retained evidence archive as appropriate. Cite the original source sections, not just DOI or abstract; inspect contrary studies, independence, selection effects, sample and validity limits. Respect privacy/publication authority for supplied personal correspondence.

A claimed scholarly-consensus reversal must state what relevant specialists actually agree or dispute, its strongest existing argument, and the novel observation, intervention, formal derivation or countermodel that changes its support. Independently check symbolic, SAT/SMT, statistical and numerical work while separating conditional mathematical validity from empirical realization. Mark `supported | qualified | unverified | contradicted | specialist review required`; unresolved load-bearing links block a claim of publishable scholarly certification. A counterexample to an unqualified universal inference does not refute every narrower version of the disputed theory.

## Direct scholarly and dialogue quotation retention gate

Before accepting any shorter or reorganized edition, execute `python scripts/evidence_retention_audit.py ORIGINAL.html CANDIDATE.html --output PRIVATE_REVIEW_QUEUE.json`. This produces a conservative mechanical review queue of quoted strings and DOI links missing or changed. Its count is **not** a count of confirmed scholarly quote errors: nested quotations, rhetorical questions, titles and attribution ambiguities need human checking. Review every candidate against the original sentence in context, the primary paper/recording/message actually attributed, and the revised argumentative location.

Maintain a private per-item ledger with the original exact quote, author/speaker, edition/date and accessible evidence, privacy or reuse constraints, what argument it bears, revised article location, exact retained or carefully corrected wording, and final `retained | corrected with verified source | quarantined pending verification/permission | author-approved omission` disposition. For a quotation from a peer-reviewed paper, compare to the paper's **full relevant passage** and record whether the study supports the claim being drawn from it; DOI metadata alone cannot do this. A supplied private conversation is not automatically permission for open publication; seek the owner's decision where necessary. Distinguish faithful quotations from constructed opponent paraphrases. Keep material verified evidence directly readable alongside the argument whenever legally and ethically possible. Preserve all original quotes and citations in the full private historical record even when a text-facing correction is warranted.

**Hard gate:** Zero material quoted passages or directly bearing peer-reviewed sources silently removed, altered or attributed without verification; unresolved cases listed for owner decision. Retest after whole-paper mergers and include the redline output in the private reconstruction ZIP. Do not claim that matching text establishes quotation authenticity, rights, factual warrant or faithful contextual interpretation.

## Four canonical foundations in every article, with complete edited HTML every pass

A completed article audit must link each official framework page **at least once** in context: [RCF methodology](https://sweetrationalism.com/frameworks/recursive-corrigibility-framework/), [PBSRR ontology](https://sweetrationalism.com/frameworks/ontology-scale-relative-realism/), [Framework Bridge](https://sweetrationalism.com/frameworks/interoperability-framework-bridge/), and [Sweet Rationalism Ethos](https://sweetrationalism.com/frameworks/sweet-rationalism-a-foundational-framework/). Prefer an actual proposition's relevant paragraph; if one page is merely background, put a compact reader-friendly “Related foundations” sentence in a suitable front/back matter location. This required four-link foundation layer is distinct from the **selective** contextual links to related articles and must not create a spammy reciprocal article network.

Link actual authorial mentions of “Recursive Corrigibility Framework,” “RCF,” “PBSRR,” “my ontology,” “Framework Bridge,” and “Sweet Rationalism Ethos” to their correct canonical destination; preserve literal participant/source quotations and previously linked text. Editorial review decides whether closely repeated links should be condensed without leaving important named concepts inaccessible. Run `python scripts/core_framework_links_gate.py ARTICLE.html --strict`: no missing one of the four page links is the structural hard gate; its unlinked-mention queue must receive human dispositions. Check signed-out target status, anchors and rendered navigation. A script pass cannot establish that the inserted relationship is honest or useful.

**Every audit pass must ship its full current edited HTML**, privately for protected articles, with pass/version/date, SHA-256, full before/after diff and evidence/audit status. The HTML must be directly accessible as its own file, even when a historical ZIP contains a copy. If unchanged since last pass, link the unchanged full file and say it is unchanged. A patch, summary, screenshot or WordPress text box alone fails this deliverable gate. Each later pass must yield a new version or explicitly attest to unchanged full bytes; do not replace historical files without provenance.

## Preflight package and release hold

Before requesting final approval, assemble **privately**:

- Complete proposed article master and requested versions, rendered and editorially checked.
- A source-grounded near-top **Bottom line** that passes the placement check and a human scholarly-term, inferential-scope, and readability review; record the check-off receipt.
- Full citation/source ledger with article-claim-to-paper-passage mappings and all unresolved full-text issues.
- Proof-obligation list, code, exact test outputs, countermodels, dependency graph, rival comparison and unproved assumptions.
- Prose detector output plus human dispositions and prose diff, and a full-article cross-section cohesion/merge decision map with protected evidence-retention receipts.
- Original and proposed HTML, WordPress revision snapshots where available, SHA-256 hashes, exact unified/adjacent diffs, reconstruction manifest and rollback procedure.
- Public-facing dated Revision Notes, proposed DOI metadata and release sequence, draft audit .txt and complete history ZIP (no fake public links).
- Sitewide public-inventory inbound/outbound link/defer/reject matrix, exact proposed contextual anchors and insertion passages, image/accessibility/Yoast/robots, citation, DOI status and independent Gemini/Antigravity cross-check receipts.
- Owner-facing summary: strongest surviving results, narrowed/withdrawn claims, open mathematical obligations, genuinely new work, and decisions requiring Nathan's judgment.

**Mandatory owner checkpoint:** Nathan reviews the exact candidate and remaining limitations. Do not submit/deposit the Zenodo article DOI, put a DOI into WordPress, publish protected archives, unlist/relist, remove noindex, alter sitemaps or declare release complete without explicit approval of this candidate and these release actions. If his review produces material changes, rerun affected checks and obtain approval of the new candidate.

## Per-work source anchoring and anti-name-dropping

- For each substantive mention of a named scholar, specific paper, external article, dialogue source or historical work, verify exactly **which publication, edition and claim** is meant and provide a natural inline link at the supporting paragraph. Prefer publisher/DOI/author original over derivative summaries. A shared author name is not paper identity.
- Link external HTTP(S) research destinations with `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`. Link canonical pages on `sweetrationalism.com` in the same tab. This is a navigation requirement, not a proven SEO ranking effect. Audit malformed redirects and duplicate anchors.
- Every current bibliography record needs an argumentative dependency, original quotation, serious rival or a meaningful primary-sourced contribution in a scholar appendix. Remove reference-only padding and decorative name lists from the live edition, retaining source identities and before/after texts in versioned archival materials for reversal.
- When a relevant scholar appears solely by name in prose, supply their actual substantive contribution, direct evidence, counterargument or a verified quotation; if there is no such connection, remove the name without removing a distinct argument. Where rival scholarship carries a direct objection and substantive published answer, present both.
- Keep a review ledger per cited work with `work_id/DOI`, exact original/present context, source-match scope, decision, stronger contrary evidence, loss condition and archive destination. Classify a punctuation-shaped author hypothetical separately from a verbatim third-party quotation. Automated `95 flags` never means `95 quotation errors`.
- The claim/quote gates, framework-link gate, archival reconstruction and owner signoff remain independent and cumulative; final release is prohibited until all required checks close.

## Interpret machine results accurately

| Automated check | What it can establish | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prose pattern | A text pattern occurs under stated extraction rules | AI origin or bad writing |
| Bibliographic lookup | DOI/metadata exist | Claim-to-paper support |
| Symbolic/dimensional check | Algebra or units under stated inputs | Correct empirical premises |
| SAT/SMT | Model or contradiction of supplied encoding | Faithful natural-language formalization |
| Enumeration/property test | Behavior over tested cases | Universal validity outside scope |
| Proof assistant | Kernel acceptance of encoded theorem/dependencies | Physical truth or external scholarly peer review |

Failures warrant local correction and retesting, not abandonment of unrelated empirical evidence. Passing checks remain revisable when a better formalization, source, or counterexample appears.
