# RCF scholarly article audit prompt (methodology-first)

Use this with AGENTS.md and article-specific instructions; stricter existing publication and privacy gates remain binding.

Audit the canonical website article against primary sources and its archived versions. Separate (A) methodological contributions, (B) empirical observations, (C) formal results, (D) hypotheses and rival models, (E) metaphysical interpretations, and (F) ethical/normative bridges. Do not let evidence for A or B automatically promote D or E. Assess whether the methodology remains useful even when its favored substantive conjecture fails.

For every major section, produce a methodology-versus-conclusion matrix: exact claim, claim type, boundary B, timescale tau, source provenance, evidential currency, strongest rival, independently measured discriminator, negative control, possible confound, false-positive/false-negative mechanism, prospective loss condition, and recommended retain/revise/demote/remove disposition. Preserve the strongest defensible original argument and its provenance; no cosmetic weakening or unsupported strengthening.

For each added posit H relative to empirically grounded baseline B0, report incremental yield ΔG in risky prediction, causal intervention, out-of-sample forecast, compression or transfer, accounting for complexity and shared evidence. Apply the same standard to favored and rival accounts. Compatibility, explanatory vocabulary, source authority and a rival's failure alone are not corroboration.

For consciousness specifically, separate the metaproblem, system-boundary problem, organizational/process problem and phenomenal-identity problem. Independently measure closure, recurrence, integration, global availability, higher-order representation, biological variables, and phenomenal evidence where possible. Do not define phenomenal outcomes using the proposed organizational predictors. Test matched externally scaffolded versus endogenously maintained systems, distinguish organizational capacity from experience, and compare specified variants of GNW, RPT, HOT, IIT, biological and enactive/active-inference theories. Predeclare how each rival can win and what result demotes the preferred model. Treat reports as calibrated evidence, not revelation or noise.

Produce: (1) section matrix; (2) typed dependency and rival graph storing claims and loss conditions, not conclusions; (3) source and quotation provenance ledger; (4) rival forecast and negative-control table; (5) prospective protocol with sample/population, preregistered primary outcomes, thresholds, power justification, exclusions, blinding, held-out scoring and correction rules; (6) executable checks with bounded interpretation; (7) full HTML candidate, exact rebuildable TXT diff, checksums and link/render checks; (8) separate methodological, source, formal, empirical and ontological dispositions; (9) explicit remaining uncertainty and next decisive test.

Distinguish prospective proposals from registered study protocols and actual corroboration. A valid code run proves only the stated bounded computation. A source check establishes only the checked citation or quotation. A DOI records a version, not validity. Preserve private source ledgers, hidden-article restrictions and immutable release provenance. Website-only date/revision notices need no new DOI; material changes require a new archived version. Apply the same tests to this audit's own claims and errors.
