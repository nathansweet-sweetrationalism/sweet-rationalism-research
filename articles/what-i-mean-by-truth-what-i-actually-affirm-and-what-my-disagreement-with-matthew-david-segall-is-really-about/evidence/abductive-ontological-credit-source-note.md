# From Abductive Advantage to Discriminating Ontological Credit

The quoted draft passage and its surrounding analysis are reproduced in the canonical article. The central methodological distinction is between (1) grounds for pursuit, (2) explanatory virtues, (3) comparative evidential support, and (4) the scope of ontological credit. These can interact, but none automatically confers all the others.
