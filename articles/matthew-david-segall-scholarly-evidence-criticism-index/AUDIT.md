# Segall scholarly evidence index: source and publication audit

Audit date: 2026-11-09. Canonical public edition: https://sweetrationalism.com/articles/theology/matthew-david-segall-scholarly-evidence-criticism-index/

## Release decision
- The index is a bibliographic and argument-mapping resource, not a peer-reviewed journal article or a new scientific experiment.
- Its sections type each source by what the source *actually* addresses. Direct peer-reviewed criticism that names a Segall work was not independently verified in the Nov 9 search; absence from this list is not proof of absence.
- Six named public/essay responses explicitly discuss Segall: Erik Haines (2), Bernardo Kastrup, Timothy Jackson, Benjamin Snyder, Jeffrey Williams. Their status is not disguised as peer review.
- Twelve peer-reviewed works relate to arguments Segall advances. None is misattributed as a direct rebuttal of Segall.
- Primary Segall sources are included to make the reconstruction and counterarguments independently accessible.
- The index links to previously published articles only; private, draft, or unlisted WordPress content remains excluded.

## Source identity checks and caveats
- McHenry and Shields (2016), DOI 10.1017/apa.2016.21, is not a straightforward anti-Whitehead refutation; it contains sympathetic analytical reconstruction as well as discussion of critiques.
- Shepherd (1974), *Process Studies* 4(1), is a criticism of Whiteheadian panpsychism predating Segall, not criticism directed at Segall.
- Hartshorne's response appears in *Process Studies* 6(1), 67–72 (1976); a full-text aggregator inconsistently labels it Winter 1974. Volume/year source has priority.
- Henry Taylor, “Is Panpsychism Simple?”, *Analysis* 79(2), 265–275 (2019), DOI 10.1093/analys/any049: publisher bibliographic attribution takes precedence over conflicting third-party portal metadata.
- Montévil and Mossio's verified 2015 DOI is 10.1016/j.jtbi.2015.02.029.
- Donahue's 2025 paper DOI 10.1007/s10670-024-00790-y has a publisher correction DOI 10.1007/s10670-024-00837-0. No material contradiction of Sweet's argument was established from that correction.
- Casali 2013 and Cogitate 2025 are tests at bounded consciousness targets, not demonstrations that Whiteheadian universal prehension is false.
- A paper supporting experiential causation (Mørch) and a Whiteheadian defense (Hartshorne) are retained as serious alternatives.

2. Inspect each linked original essay section-by-section for citation transcription and claim-source mismatches; change original published editions only when a discrepancy has been independently established, and preserve correction logs.
3. Reassess as new studies or revisions arrive, storing claims, rival hypotheses, dependent arguments, and explicit loss conditions rather than a final-conclusions list.
4. Maintain the WordPress edition as canonical. Keep the full GitHub mirror and metadata synchronized after every public revision. Do not invent article-specific Zenodo DOIs; mint/link only a real deposit.
5. Keep the human-readable research-record links in the article body; maintain an explicit concise WordPress excerpt to prevent archival boilerplate in cards and SERP summaries.
