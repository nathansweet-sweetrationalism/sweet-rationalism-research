## Full-section audit disposition

- **Opening, abstract, methodology:** Now explicitly state independent labels, differentiated phenomenal/engineering targets, prospective tests and the distinction between pre-data proposal and completed registration.
- **I (thermodynamics, constraints and biology):** Existing conditional Landauer/closure reasoning retained. No reversion to universal `kT ln 2` per distinction or to the assertion that physical causal accounting proves exhaustive metaphysical physicalism.
- **II (phenomenality and identity):** Strengthened explanatory stopping symmetry while preserving Chalmers's actual modal arguments as serious counterpressure.
- **III (language, selfhood):** Added the residual test to prevent a grammatical critique from deleting first-person phenomena by fiat.
- **IV, IV-A, IV-B (bridges and Whitehead):** Retained local source/construct distinctions; added conditional positive phenomenal powers and matched whole-account rivals rather than conflating process metaphysics with a detachable hypothesis.
- **V (panpsychism, Levin, quantum, IIT, GNW, active inference):** Retained original target-specific criticisms. Added the explicit warning that IIT, enaction and active inference can predict the same causal topology contrast as RPOC. No universal rival refutation follows from a failed local signature.
- **VI (clinical and comparative evidence):** Added outcome-label independence, blinded provenance, diagnostic ambiguity and participant safeguards.
- **VII (culture, first-person practice, language models):** Retained; culturally variable explanatory judgments motivate the separate metaproblem proposal, without deciding ontology.
- **VIII (self-application and loss):** Added label leakage, no incremental gain and omitted phenomenal residual as separable ways specific claims can lose support.
- **IX (test programme):** New prospective joint fingerprints, hysteresis, selective disconnection, engineered loop/yoked controls separated from human phenomenology, boundary interventions, metaproblem protocol and study-specific registration gates.
- **X (substrate):** Retained: non-biological implementation is possible in principle on these hypotheses, not presumed conscious; neither biology nor computation supplies sufficiency by designation.
- **XI (implications):** Added conditional dissolution of a further problem-form. Ethical and clinical protection does not depend on resolving phenomenal ontology.
- **XII (formal):** Formal models remain bounded; strengthened label contamination and typed loss requirements. They cannot prove phenomenality. The published Brier example is an illustrative exact arithmetic identity, not an observed predictive improvement.
- **Appendices A–D:** Preserved serious modal and scientific rival space; added label provenance, dependence-aware scoring and claim/loss graph instructions.
- **Revision Notes and References:** Note methodology update, prior source-review closure and prospective-only status; retain one end-of-document References section and author-hosted primary Chalmers links.

## Empirical programme status

P1 recovery hysteresis, P2 perspectival disconnection, P3 endogenous loop versus yoked correction, P4 boundary/topology and P5 the metaproblem have versioned proposal JSONs in `prereg_proposals/` and a readable `PROSPECTIVE_PROTOCOL.md`. All five satisfied the structural contract validator, which does **not** test scientific adequacy. A sample-size/power justification, named cohort or apparatus, independent rival sign-off, fixed endpoint, detailed exclusion/analysis decisions and study-registration ID remain missing. No outcomes have been selected or tested here.

**Most discriminating prospective result:** reproducible incremental, held-out, intervention-sensitive prediction of independently calibrated experience-linked states from endogenous self/world/action regulation where frozen rival models predict otherwise. Equivalent predictive performance or better causal explanation by a simpler rival demotes the distinctive-marker claim. A model without independent phenomenal targets can still establish engineering value but cannot win the phenomenal conclusion.
