02 · Common questions and the criticisms behind them

Is Michael Levin’s work legitimate science or pseudoscience?

Bioelectricity and morphogenesis are legitimate experimental research. The questions collected here concern particular measurements, interpretations and extensions: when control counts as cognition, when cognition supports consciousness, and what identifies an independent Platonic cause. These claims need different evidence.

Unexpected behavior, useful mathematics and productive research deserve their own credit. They do not automatically establish a nonphysical source, a conscious subject or a safe therapeutic or AI application. The same standard applies to my proposed alternatives and my computational audits.

Abraham Wald & Jacob Wolfowitz · Annals of Mathematical Statistics · 1940

Softmax: where the biological analogy has to pay its evidential bill

  • Cooperation with whom? Agents can cooperate superbly with one another while excluding or exploiting humans. A cohesive collective is not automatically a humane one.
  • What happens when humans are no longer useful? If cooperation depends on mutual need, test what survives changes in bargaining power, access to resources and dependence.
  • What is the engineered mechanism? Specify the AI counterpart of biological coupling, boundaries, error signals and regulation. Shared biological vocabulary cannot substitute for a demonstrated implementation.
  • What counts as care? Distinguish observable welfare protection from inferred subjective concern. Conscious experience is neither established by cooperative behavior nor required as a premise for testing safety.
  • What survives outside the training game? Test unfamiliar agents, hostile incentives, coalitions, deception, resource asymmetries and changing environments. Cooperation within a simulation does not certify deployment safety.
  • Who corrects the collective? Demand evidence for oversight, correction, containment and continued human influence. More effective collective problem solving can increase capabilities before it establishes protection.
