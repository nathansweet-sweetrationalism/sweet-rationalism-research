About the image above. Satirical illustration, not a record of events. The stamp stands for a constant label in Dietz’s validator that prints VERIFIED whatever error it is given. The 14/14 and 34.11% sheets are real entries in his saved benchmark and figure; the 900% sheet is my synthetic negative control, not a measured result. The green NEGATIVE RESULT label on the 0% panel is my addition, not the validator’s output: the 0% is Dietz’s own judgment score, which I accept on other grounds.
