### 2026-11-09 — DOI badge layout correction after rendered-site review

**Observed regression:** An earlier audit note proposed inline DOI badges, contrary to the owner's preferred badge-below-URL design. A subsequent correction placed one `<br>` before and another after each badge, which produced visibly empty rows. Read-only inspection of the live Kadence-rendered page established the mechanism: the badge `img` has `display:block`, so the following `<br>` created an unnecessary extra line. The earlier inline-placement instruction in this log is superseded, not erased.

**Final current rule:** Use visible full clickable resolver URL; one `<br>` **before** its corresponding Zenodo badge; **no `<br>` after** the badge; next metadata entry starts on the following natural line. Both version and concept DOIs follow the same pattern. Keep the `Research record` and `Versions and archives` paragraphs immediately adjacent, with no extra blank paragraph or margin, while preserving the DOI-to-badge and metadata line separations. Apply the rendering rule to all eligible currently DOI-bearing and future pages. This is a site/theme-specific rendering condition, not a general HTML law.

**Live edits and read-back:** WordPress post 2098 and pages 786, 2675, 1149, 2934, 11 were edited without changing their public status, DOI targets or archival labels. Each was re-read and compared byte-for-byte to the intended saved content. Across these six records, all 18 badges retain the `<br>` immediately before the badge and zero retain a trailing `<br>` after the badge. The first post's research-to-versions block retains `margin:0` on the research paragraph. The actual public DOM of post 2098 was checked after the correction and contains only the intended two line breaks in its two-DOI archive paragraph.

**Regression test:** In read-back WordPress HTML assert that each owned DOI URL precedes its matching badge, that the count of `<br><a class="sr-doi-badge"` equals the badge count, and that no badge close `</a>` is immediately followed by another `<br>`.
