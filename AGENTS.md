27. **Methodology-first, conclusion-provisional audit rule (2026-11-10):** Evaluate the transferable, operational, self-correcting methodology independently of any favored empirical or metaphysical conclusion. Explicitly type claims as observation, inference, hypothesis, model, norm, or ontology; specify boundary, timescale, measurement, provenance, serious rivals, dependencies, negative controls, false-positive and false-negative risks, and prospective loss/update conditions. Never promote organizational closure, a successful computational test, a conceptual possibility argument, or failure of a rival to evidence of phenomenal identity without an independent bridge. For each proposed addition H to grounded baseline B0, assess incremental out-of-sample predictive, intervention, compression or transfer yield versus simpler rivals; demote unsupported conclusions while retaining useful methods. Distinguish source verification, code correctness, protocol completeness, preregistration, empirical corroboration, and metaphysical interpretation. State what was tested, what remains untested, and which result would reverse each claim. The audit prompt must explicitly request a section-by-section methodology-versus-conclusion matrix, rival forecast table, dependence-aware claim graph, prospective loss criteria and separate methodological and substantive dispositions. Do not turn a dated demonstration into permanent framework authority. Apply these requirements to the audit itself, preserving failures and uncertainty rather than manufacturing closure. Website-only publication dates and revision notices do not by themselves require a new immutable DOI or research-repository snapshot; substantive changes do.
