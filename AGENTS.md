16. **Human-readable prose gate, every article:** Apply `docs/ARTICLE_QUALITY_GATES.md` and, where executable, `scripts/prose_audit.py` to the complete candidate edition. Inspect recurrent antithetical formulas ("not X but Y" and reversals), triads, mirrored syntax, abstract-noun stacks, generic throat-clearing, repetitive section summaries, canned transitions, over-signposting, monotonous cadence, inflated claims, needless rhetorical questions, and near-duplicate paragraphs. These are *editorial leads, not AI-authorship detectors*. Count context-local recurrence; preserve exact dialogue, purposeful argument contrasts, source quotations, technical enumerations and Nathan's distinctive justified satire. The owner receives a prose-quality report with remaining justified exceptions before publication.
17. **Formal mathematics, computation and logic gate, every technical article:** Apply `docs/ARTICLE_QUALITY_GATES.md` to each lemma, theorem, equation, statistical/model claim, algorithm and inference. Check logical consequence by derivation and countermodel search; test mathematical identities, degenerate cases, physical realizability and code using deterministic reproducible methods, seeded tests and independent approaches where possible. Use `scripts/logic_audit.py` for explicitly translated classical propositional claims; use SAT/SMT, symbolic algebra, dimensional analysis, enumeration, property tests, theorem provers or proof assistants for richer logics when their actual scopes fit. Independently review every prose-to-formula translation. Label `proved`, `machine-checked in stated formalization`, `counterexample`, `tested on bounded cases`, `proposed`, `unverified` distinctly; never call a proof sketch or solver run peer review or promote a physical model from pure formal validity. Keep scripts, versions, input, stdout, failed checks and a human-readable proof obligation list private for protected articles until release.

19. **Mandatory near-top “Bottom line” for every fully audited article:** Following any existing reader-facing `Research record` / `Versions and archives` references, and before the abstract or main argument, include a short paragraph beginning **`Bottom line.`** (normally about 80–160 words; substance governs length). Model its structure on the actual published [Michael Levin Platonic Morphospace: The Ingression Challenge](https://sweetrationalism.com/articles/biology/bioelectricity-morphogenesis/absence-is-possibility-space-a-recursive-constraint-falsification-analysis-of-michael-levins-platonic-morphospace-hypothesis-platonic-space-discussion-3/): give the article's particular disputed proposition, what the evidence really supports, its discriminating question or central inferential gap, the strongest relevant rival, and the important boundary on what has **not** been refuted or established. Integrate **relevant, accurately attributed scholars and technical terms** naturally, such as Popperian falsifiability, Antony Flew's qualifications challenge, Deborah Mayo's severe testing, Lakatosian programme appraisal, Kleiner–Hoel substitution, or whatever terms actually matter for that specific article. Link authoritative SEP/IEP definitions, primary papers, DOI records or verified scholarly research where useful. These exact conceptual anchors should help human readers and computational discovery systems find the surrounding literature, competing definitions, and counterarguments; **never stuff unrelated names, imply endorsement, invent citations, or promise a search-ranking benefit**. Use a pointed, readable opening, not a formulaic list or a predetermined verdict. Include `Bottom line present, placed correctly, fact-checked, scholarship anchored, and proportionate` as a **hard check-off in the per-article audit and final release package**; test its placement with `scripts/bottom_line_gate.py` when available and record a separate human semantic/source review. The checker is structural only and passing it cannot certify truth or relevance.

20. **Mandatory full-article and cross-section cohesion review:** Perform both a within-section audit and a second end-to-end audit of the **entire** article (including appendices, notes, references and transitions). Build a section-role/claim-dependency map: unique contribution, premise, evidence, inferential bridge, strongest rival, criticism and harm/risk argument, qualifications and loss conditions. Cluster recurring propositions **across distant sections** even if paraphrased and no lexical near-duplicate exists. Identify repeated conclusions, caveats, rhetorical patterns, overlong recaps, buried arguments, headings and sections that can be merged or reordered. For each candidate compression record original passages and section IDs, suggested combined placement, exact claims/premises/defeaters preserved, references/quotations preserved, estimated navigation/readability gain, risk and decision `merge | trim | move | retain with reason | unresolved`. Never delete a distinct conclusion, supporting inference, critical counterargument or material harm warning to meet a word-count goal. Scan and read the **whole revised edition again**, not only edited sections; preserve separate before/after diffs and a cross-section coverage receipt. A per-section pass without global review is an incomplete audit.

22. **Adversarial peer-review and evidence-preservation gate:** Reconstruct **every material inference** with exact original wording, type and scope, necessary premises, conclusion, alternative explanations, a plausible countermodel or failure case, and the strongest directly relevant peer-reviewed primary literature. For empirical generalizations seek independent replications, source-to-passage confirmation, methods, sample, effect sizes, uncertainties, dependencies, contrary findings and retractions; distinguish original experiments from their commentary. Claims that challenge a scholarly consensus require a faithful statement of that consensus, a genuinely discriminating primary-evidence or proof-based challenge, reproducible calculation/code where appropriate, and honest outstanding tests; formal validity cannot establish empirical premises or metaphysical identity. Label `supported | qualified | unverified | contradicted | requires external specialist review` instead of manufacturing a verdict. **Preserve every directly argument-bearing peer-reviewed source, exact verified source quotation, and user-provided personal/public dialogue quotation, along with attribution, surrounding context, source/version, and privacy/publication status.** Do not condense an argument by deleting a relevant source, a distinct premise, a rival or a harm/ethical warning; compress repeated commentary around protected evidence. If a quote is unverifiable, sensitive, disputed, copyright-limited or lacks publication authority, quarantine and flag it for the owner without inventing a substitute or leaking it publicly. Record before/after and the explicit retention decision. A hostile critique may weaken a specific inference, never assume it disproves the whole position. No full-audit completion without source/premise coverage and owner review.
